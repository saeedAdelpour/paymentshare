# Payment Share

Shared expenses and repayments among members of a workspace.

## Language

**Workspace**:
A shared space for members to track expenses and debts in one currency. A user can belong to multiple workspaces.

**Member**:
A user who belongs to a workspace.

**Removed member**:
A former workspace member whose history and balance are retained. Their access is read-only and limited to their own balance and repayment suggestions involving them, excluding transaction history.

**Owner**:
The member responsible for a workspace who can grant permissions to other members.

**Service administrator**:
A person authorized to access records across all workspaces to operate Payment Share.
_Avoid_: Owner when referring to administration of the whole service

**Staff user**:
A user authorized to use the administration panel within their granted administrative permissions.

**Superuser**:
A staff user with all administrative permissions without needing individual permission grants.

**Deactivated user**:
A user whose access to Payment Share is disabled across all workspaces while their identity, memberships, and history are retained.

**Expense**:
An amount paid by one member on behalf of selected members. The payer shares the expense only when included among those members.
_Avoid_: Transaction when specifically referring to an expense

**Repayment**:
A record of money paid outside the app by one member to another toward settling debt. It may be less than, equal to, or greater than the amount owed.
_Avoid_: Payment when it could imply the app transfers money

**Creator**:
The user who records an expense or repayment.

**Payer**:
The member who paid an expense.

**Share**:
The portion of an expense allocated to a selected member.

**Net balance**:
The aggregate amount a member is owed or owes within one workspace after expenses and repayments are accounted for.

**Simplified debts**:
Suggested amounts members should pay one another based on their workspace net balances. These may connect different members from those involved in the original expenses, without changing the expense or repayment history.

**Void**:
Cancel an expense or repayment's effect on balances while retaining its history.

**Revision**:
A retained version of an expense or repayment that records its values and who changed it. Direct edits preserve earlier revisions.
